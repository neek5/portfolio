Overview

Taking a leap of faith after completing my diploma, I joined Google for a year-long traineeship that fundamentally shaped my career trajectory in cloud technology. Beginning with a rigorous three-month training program with Agilitics, I bridged the gap between theory and practice, ultimately earning my Google Professional Cloud Architect certification.

Over the course of the year, I balanced cross-functional technical projects with personal milestones, including competing in two powerlifting meets and consuming an unquantifiable amount of the office's PPP coffee.

Key Technical Projects

AWS to GCP Compute Cost Automation Tool

The Challenge: Customer Engineers (CEs) faced the highly manual, time-consuming task of comparing disparate cloud costs—akin to comparing grocery prices across stores with entirely different units, currencies, and languages.

The Solution: I developed an automated tool to streamline the direct comparison of AWS and GCP compute costs.

The Impact: This tool empowered CEs with instant instance mapping, which boosted productivity by saving 50-70% of their time and directly contributed to securing more customer deals. Furthermore, I enhanced data-driven decision-making by acquiring and deploying Apps Script for workflow optimization.

GENnifer: AI-Powered Seasonal Color Analysis

The Challenge: Professional color analysis is often expensive and inaccessible. I wanted to actively expand my skillset by solving this real-world problem through a collaborative, machine-learning-driven project.

The Solution: Partnering with a fellow intern, we built GENnifer. We leveraged Google's Gemini Pro 1.5 model and Vertex AI to analyze user selfies and extract personalized, dominant color palettes.

The Impact: We successfully built and deployed the core analytical engine. Through this process, I gained invaluable, hands-on debugging experience when navigating environmental deployment challenges, specifically troubleshooting local versus Cloud Run executions for an experimental "drape" visualization feature.
